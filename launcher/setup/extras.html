Digimon World 2 extras

Adds the features that use Digimon World 2 models: riding Unimon (F2 → Extras → Mount).

Drop your Digimon World 2 disc image here
  • You need your own copy of Digimon World 2 (PlayStation) as a .bin or .iso file. Checked with the USA release (SLUS-01193).
  • Have a .cue file? Pick the .bin file next to it.
  • The models are read and converted on this computer, in a few seconds. Nothing is downloaded.
