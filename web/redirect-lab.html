Counter redirects

Fifteen npc ids are not people. They are spriteless position markers standing where a counter clerk, a guard or a checkpoint looks reachable from — and the field confirm handler quietly swaps the marker id for the character behind it (F0x8008E2CC, table at the redirect table) before the talk arms run. That is why two records share one tile, and why the Explorer shows a marker with no sprite next to a clerk with one.

Each row below is one pair: the rooms where both ids stand in the same cast, the marker's spot (it has no sprite) and the clerk's spot and sprite. The lookup box answers the one question the Explorer cannot: press here, who do I reach?
