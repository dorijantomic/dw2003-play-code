Aspect planner & exploit audit

Measured timeline: Silver (the gym's element drills) opens at about Lv 8-12; element chips and the best gear are post-story shops. Gates are set from a typical build; the exploit audit uses the best-case drills. Proposes an element route for every extra form (an element bonus gate, the same kind the disc uses for SkullGreymon and friends) and checks how early a player could reach it by the cheapest legal means: gym element drills (limited by training points, 5 per level), element chips (limited by money) and a starting pack bonus. Nothing here changes the game: it only shows what the numbers would be.

Pace of each line

The starter's growth_xp sets how much EXP each level costs (higher = slower). Battles are estimated from the EXP of battles at the level you are playing at. Training points come from levels, so a fast line banks them in fewer battles.

Fighting strength against the enemies (the power check)

For each tier at its intended level: the player's stored stats from level-ups (each line), plus the median stat bonus of a form of that tier, plus the gear that line can wear and afford (the main-story shops, budget split across the five slots; or the flat bonus), against the median stat total of the enemies in the maps of that level (walkable pools from stage_encounters). A ratio far above the limit means that tier arrives too strong for where it sits; move its intended level later. A rough comparison: it ignores HP, techniques and the real battle formula.

Power at the earliest arrival (the real "too strong too early" test)

The worst case: the first level at which any build on any line learns a form of the tier (the simulation, tools/simulate_aspect_unlocks.mjs; set the sliders above to its numbers). Power there = the line's stored stats + the tier's form bonus + the gear that line can wear and afford, against the median enemy of that level.

How early each tier can be reached

Median over all forms of a tier. "Typical gate" is what an ordinary build holds at the intended level; "focused" puts every training point on that element with the normal drills (the exploit to guard against); "Platinum" assumes the best drill variants, whose timing in the story is not known.
