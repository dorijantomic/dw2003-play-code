Arrival Lab

Why did this place start this scene, and why not the other one? Every place's entry overlay installs a gate object that runs its own ordered candidate chain once per field load: the first candidate whose whole conjunction holds starts its event, and at most one event starts. This page lists all 78 gate objects over 76 places with their 133 candidates — 76 on the reset band and s795's and s800's second object on phase 1 — each candidate's event id, its conditions in plain words ("beat 13", "flag 16397 = 0") and the wstag citation the extractor recorded.

The answer line is the engine's own chooseArrivalEvent(chain, test); this page never decides by itself. It changes no game runtime and writes nothing.

EVERY GATE OBJECT THE FIELD INSTALLS

The chains are the disc's own, lifted read-only into assets/data/scene/arrival_candidates.json by tools/extract_arrival_candidates.py and proven against the original overlays by tests/lane-b/scene-arrival.test.js. The decision procedure is engine/systems/scene-arrival.ts. A gate object is only asked on the turn its own phase band puts it on — the reset band runs on phase 0, negative or 4 and up; the phase-1 band only on phase 1 — so this page shows the decision as the field load makes it. Ordinal leaves are the master story beat (0x6000 | beat), not flags.
