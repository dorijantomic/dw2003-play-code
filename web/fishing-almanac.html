The angler's almanac

Every fishing stage, the spots on it, and what the line can pull out. A strike does not give an item: it starts a battle. Band one takes the stage's slot 4 — a Cardmon on all forty-six stages, the one whose kill can pay a booster. Band two takes slot 7, the area's big water Digimon. A visit starts with sixteen casts, and stepping onto the map again refills them. This page only prints the shipped tables: it changes no fishing rule, no catch table and no reward.

A row is one fishing stage. Spots counts the standable tiles the stage's own step records carry. Pays is the battle-reward record at that catch's screen id, straight from progression.json — a screen the table does not reach prints unknown, never a made-up zero. Tick Cardmon only to keep the guaranteed catch in view, or pick a water Digimon to jump to the stages that hook it.
