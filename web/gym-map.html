Gym map — the nine gyms and the rank ladder each one opens

Every training gym's greeter checks your ID cards in the order below. The first rung you satisfy decides which rank menu opens, and each rank is its own drill list. Pick the gym whose high rank trains the stat you are after instead of guessing which town has it. This page only prints the ladder: it does not open the gym and does not read your bag.

A rung lists the cards it needs; must not have means that card sends you to an earlier rung. One rung is checked after another, so the first fully satisfied line is the menu you get. A drill's second core stat is a loss chance, while a second element or max HP/MP row is another gain. TP is the training points you spend, chosen on the gym screen.
