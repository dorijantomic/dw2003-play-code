digimon world 3 · your memory card

Save X-ray

Pick one of your three memory-card slots and read it as the disc's own 0x26C4-byte block: BIT, the 402 carried and 402 worn item counts, the eight roster records with their 44 form cells, the 315 card counts, the three 40-card folders, the 13 stored flag banks and the play clock. Every offset is named. Every byte outside a named interval is kept and labelled retained — the layout does not give those bytes a meaning, so neither does this page.

Pick two slots and the right-hand list shows only the fields that differ: what changed between my two saves. The bytes come from the card the game wrote, not from a fresh guess.

WHAT CHANGED

The layout and every population come from the engine's own save-block constants and the walker's docs/research/systems/save-block-layout.md. "Retained" means the disc stores the byte losslessly and the layout gives it no meaning yet — not that it is empty. Nothing on this page writes to a save.
