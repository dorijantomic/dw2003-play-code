Extra digivolution forms

The 135 enemy-only Digimon that each of the eight starters can learn as a form, after the disc's own 44. Pick a starter line to see when it unlocks each one and how strong it is; click a column header to sort. "Depth" is how many prerequisite forms deep the unlock is (0 = no prerequisite). Gate: a stat the starter must have reached, or the line's own level.
